E4: learning from samples; regret is exact (oracle) and relative to the optimal cost.

Each agent learns each of 20 held-out maps from scratch (3 seeds per map, 3,000,000 steps). Regret is heavy-tailed (a policy that loops pays until the horizon), so the median and quartiles are shown next to the mean. A run 'loops' when its final policy times out with probability above 0.5%. 'Learned' means regret at most 5% of the optimum.

Generalisation. A linear learner trained for 1,000,000 steps across the 20 training maps (5 seeds), then scored with no further samples on the held-out maps: regret 18.7% [8.7 to 48.2] (mean 42%), 0 of 100 policies loop, P(fail) 3.2%.

Q-learning matches that per-map regret within 3,000,000 steps on 15 of 20 maps; median steps to match: 1,560,000.
